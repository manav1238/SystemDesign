# Learning Status Tracker

Tracks study status for the HLD Core Concept files, grouped by topic area. Use this to decide **what to study next**; use `../Revision/` for rapid recall once a concept is **Learning → Revised**.

## Status Legend

- **Learning** — authored and verified, needs your review/study.
- **Revised** — you have actively studied this concept (active recall passed).

## How to Use

1. Pick a topic area below, read files in order, then close them and attempt Section 22 (Active Recall).
